### Outline

1. **Introduction**
   - Custom software development costs vary widely because every project is scoped differently.
   - Unlike off-the-shelf software, custom development is priced around your specific requirements, not a fixed license fee.
   - This guide explains what shapes cost, what to expect at different scope levels, and how to get a realistic estimate.

2. **What Shapes Custom Software Development Cost**
   - Project scope and number of features/screens
   - Third-party integrations and API connections
   - Authentication, user roles, and access controls
   - Data complexity and dashboard requirements
   - Design depth and interaction requirements
   - Testing scope and quality assurance
   - Deployment environment and hosting
   - Ongoing maintenance and support

3. **Typical Scope Ranges (Conceptual)**
   - Landing page / simple brochure site
   - Business website with multiple sections
   - Web application with user accounts and data management
   - Full-scale platform with integrations and automation
   - Note: No fixed prices are stated. Each project is unique.

4. **How to Get an Accurate Estimate**
   - Define your core requirements before reaching out
   - Share existing systems, tools, or APIs you need to connect
   - Describe your timeline and launch expectations
   - StackSpacer provides a project-specific estimate after the discovery phase

5. **When Custom Software Makes Sense**
   - Off-the-shelf tools don't fit your workflow
   - You need systems that integrate with your existing stack
   - Your business processes are unique and shouldn't be generalized
   - Long-term ownership and scalability matter more than short-term cost

6. **FAQ**
   - How long does the estimating process take?
   - Do you need a detailed brief before contacting StackSpacer?
   - Can you work with an existing budget?
   - What happens after the estimate is provided?
